How it works

Three steps, no guesswork about whether a part actually fits your car.

First time takes a minute. After that, your car is remembered.

Why it's different

Every other platform we looked at does one of these. Vinzuu does all three.

Exact-fit matching

Matched to your car's generation, engine and build market, not just the model name.

  • Decoded from your VIN
  • No more "almost fits" parts

Real price comparison

See what every shop near you charges for the same part before you buy.

  • Sorted by price and distance
  • No price hidden until checkout

Verified, not just listed

A shop only earns the top trust label once we've checked it. A listing alone never does.

  • Genuine vs. compatible, labeled
  • Unverified listings flagged, not hidden

How we keep listings honest

Anyone can list a part as "compatible." Vinzuu only shows the top trust label once it's actually been checked against your car's build.

  • Every part number is checked against generation, engine and build market before it's shown as a match.
  • If we're not sure a part fits, we say so — we don't just hide it or guess.
  • Shops earn verification; they don't buy it.
