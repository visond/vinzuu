Vinzuu Sellers & Garages

Sell parts or fit them, with customers who know their car

Every customer on Vinzuu searches by VIN, so they only see the parts that fit their car. Join as a parts shop, a garage, or both.

  1. 1
    ApplyFill in the form below with your RDB business details and two photos.
  2. 2
    We check your businessThe vinzuu team checks your documents and may call or visit you.
  3. 3
    Start workingSign in with your email and open your dashboard.

For parts shops

  • We post your parts for you, checked against verified part numbers, so customers trust them.
  • You keep your own prices and stock up to date.
  • Customers reserve parts and reach you on WhatsApp.
  • Ratings and reviews from real customers.

For garages

  • Customers who bought a part choose you to fit it.
  • Accept a job with a date and time, then mark it done.
  • Show your labour prices, so customers know the cost before they come.
  • Ratings and reviews from real customers.
